# Ticket／狀態句型庫（去密通用）

來源：日常回信語氣（不含密碼、價格、個資）。

## 開場

- Hi [Name],
- Got it.
- Update on [ID]:

## 現象

- Users reported that [URL] is down.
- On my side I get: [error text]
- I can see [fact].

## 請對方做

- Can you open this site on your side?
- Can you check whether the issue reproduces on your side as well?
- Could you share the exact issue details, replication steps, and test data?
- Please let me know once [X] has been created.

## 狀態

- still Work In Progress with [Name]
- The ticket remains with [Name] for further investigation.
- It was acknowledged the same night.
- We deployed the fix to the test server yesterday and are waiting for confirmation.

## 暫行

- Until further notice, please use [workaround].
- Until this is fixed, please use [workaround] for now.

## 結尾

- Thank you
